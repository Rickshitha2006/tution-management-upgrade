Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The system runs on Cloudflare’s free plans, so there is no subscription. Extremely large academies could eventually exceed the free usage limits, but a typical tuition centre never will.

Where is my data stored?

In your own private account on Cloudflare’s secure cloud database. Each academy’s records are kept separate and are only visible after you sign in.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to phones, tablets and computers, and you can install it on your home screen like an app. An internet connection is needed to save changes.

Can I track my teachers and pay salaries?

Yes. Add each staff member with a monthly salary, mark their attendance daily, and the salary sheet calculates pay from attendance. You can add bonuses and advances and download payslips.

Can I get my data out?

Always. Download a full backup, export students and fees to Excel or CSV, and generate PDF reports at any time.

What if I forget my password?

Keep your login details somewhere safe, and take regular backups from the Settings page so your records are always protected.
