If you forget your password, the recovery code lets you create a new one from the login page. If you lose both your password and your recovery code, your account cannot be recovered. Making a new code stops the old one from working.

Move to a New Phone

Easiest way: Install Smart Academy System on the new phone and log in with the same email and password — your students, attendance and fees will already be there.

Manual backup: Settings → Backup All Data on the old phone, then Settings → Restore from Backup on the new one.
